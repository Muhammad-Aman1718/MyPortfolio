## Introduction

The first launch is the moment an app is most likely to be abandoned. Performance work usually targets measured load time, but users react to perceived readiness — the moment the screen looks usable — which can differ from technical completion by a wide margin.

## Method

4,000 recorded first-launch sessions were annotated with the moment each screen became visually ready and the moment the user acted or left. Sessions were grouped by perceived wait to find where abandonment rises sharply.

## Results

Abandonment stayed roughly flat for short perceived waits and rose steeply beyond about 1.8 seconds. Skeleton screens and immediate visual feedback moved sessions below the threshold even when the underlying load time didn't change.

## Recommendations

- Budget **perceived readiness**, not only total load time: aim for a usable-looking first screen within 1.8 seconds.
- Show layout immediately with skeletons; fill content as it arrives.
- Defer permission prompts and account creation until after the first moment of value.
