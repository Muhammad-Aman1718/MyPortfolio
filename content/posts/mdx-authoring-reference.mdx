### A sub-heading

Every `##` heading becomes a section in the table of contents, with a stable anchor you can link to.

## Embeds

Use `<YouTube id="VIDEO_ID" title="What the video shows" />` with the 11-character id from the video URL. It loads from youtube-nocookie.com and renders nothing if the id is invalid.
